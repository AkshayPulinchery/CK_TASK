Privacy Policy

CIVICFORCE Data Protection Charter

1. Information collected: Problem description, coordinates, landmark, up to 3 photos, optional name/phone/email.

2. Contact privacy: Citizen contact details are NEVER shown on public challenge pages or maps. Only authorized signed-in administrative reviewers can view them.

3. AI processing: Automated AI triage runs without transmitting personal identities.

Terms of Service

Usage Guidelines

1. Truthful submissions: Users must submit authentic, first-hand civic issues.

2. No abuse: Defamation or leaking personal details of other citizens is strictly forbidden.

3. Moderation: Reviewers reserve the right to refine labels or reject non-conforming entries.
